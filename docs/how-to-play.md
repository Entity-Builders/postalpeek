# 🗺️ PostalPeek — How to Play

**Collect postcards from around the world, painted by an AI artist who walks real streets.**

---

## The Story

**Kyle Walker** is a digital artist who roams the world 24/7. Every street, every café, every hidden mural he encounters becomes a hand-painted watercolor postcard. Your mission? **Collect them all.**

---

## 🃏 The Basics

### 1. Browse the Feed
Scroll through Walker's latest postcards — each one is a real place, illustrated in his signature watercolor style.

### 2. Claim Postcards
See a postcard you love? Tap **"Claim"** to add it to your collection. Every postcard is unique — **only one person can own each one**. First come, first served.

### 3. Daily Pack
Come back every day to open your **Daily Pack** — 3 free postcards waiting to be revealed. It's like opening a fresh pack of trading cards.

---

## 📬 Your Collection

All your claimed postcards live in **My Collection**. Browse them, admire them, and track your progress toward completing albums.

---

## 📖 Albums

Albums are themed challenges curated by Walker based on what he discovers on his trips.

**Examples:**
- 🏟️ *Estadios de Capital Federal* — Collect all 12 stadium postcards
- 🎨 *Street Art de Palermo* — Find 20 murals around the neighborhood
- ☕ *Cafés Notables de Buenos Aires* — 15 legendary coffee spots
- 🌎 *Vuelta al Mundo* — One postcard from every country Walker visits

**How they work:**
- Each album has a set number of **slots** to fill
- Claim postcards that match an album's theme to fill its slots
- Missing postcards appear as **silhouettes** — so you know what to look for
- Complete an album → earn a **badge** and unlock **bonus claims**

---

## ⭐ Rarity

Not all postcards are created equal. Walker assigns rarity based on what he finds:

| Rarity | How it happens |
|---|---|
| **Common** | Everyday streets and corners |
| **Rare** | Interesting architecture, cool street art |
| **Epic** | Iconic landmarks, golden hour lighting |
| **Legendary** | Once-in-a-lifetime moments — a festival, a rare sunset |

## 🔄 Trading *(Coming Soon)*

Found a postcard someone else needs? They have one you want?

- Propose a **1-for-1 trade** directly from their profile
- If they accept → the postcards swap owners instantly
- Build your collection faster by trading with the community

---

## 📸 Verified Claims *(Coming Soon)*

Been to the actual place in a postcard you own? **Prove it.**

1. Open any claimed postcard and tap **"Verify"**
2. Take a photo of the real location
3. Our AI compares your shot with Walker's original
4. Match? You earn the **✅ Verified** badge on that postcard

**Why verify?**
- Your photo replaces the original on the postcard's back — it becomes *yours*
- Verified postcards are more valuable in trades
- Complete an album with all postcards verified → **"Explorer" badge**
